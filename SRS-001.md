## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose
This document aims to fully and systematically specify the software requirements of the **ExpenseManager** system, responsible for managing employee corporate expenses. It serves as a baseline for development, testing, validation, and maintenance activities.

### 1.2 Scope
ExpenseManager is a full-stack system that enables the registration, control, approval, and auditing of employee corporate expenses, including the enforcement of business rules, generation of alerts, and production of managerial reports. This document covers the entire system, including back-end, front-end, and required integrations.

### 1.3 Definitions, Acronyms, and Abbreviations
- **SRS**: Software Requirements Specification
- **CRUD**: Create, Read, Update, Delete
- **API**: Application Programming Interface
- **MTBF**: Mean Time Between Failures
- **MTTR**: Mean Time To Repair

### 1.4 Overview
This document is organized into sections that first present an overall view of the system and then detail the specific functional and non-functional requirements, as well as interfaces, constraints, and applicable standards.

## 2. Overall Description

### 2.1 Product Perspective
ExpenseManager replaces manual processes based on spreadsheets and email exchanges, providing a centralized, auditable, and secure solution for corporate expense management.

### 2.2 Product Functions
- Employee expense registration
- Automatic enforcement of spending limits
- Hierarchical approval workflow
- Alert generation and management
- Managerial reporting

### 2.3 User Characteristics
- **Employee**: Operational users who submit expenses
- **Manager**: First-level approvers
- **Finance**: Final approvers and department budget managers

### 2.4 Constraints
- Mandatory use of Java 21 and Spring Boot 3.x for the back-end
- Mandatory use of React with TypeScript for the front-end
- Use of a relational database

### 2.5 Assumptions and Dependencies
- Authentication based on mock username and password
- Execution environment available either locally or in the cloud

## 3. Specific Requirements

### 3.1 Functionality

#### FR01 – User Authentication
The system shall allow user authentication through username and password (mock), with role-based access control.

#### FR02 – Employee Management
The system shall allow the creation, retrieval, update, and deletion of employee records, including association with departments and managers.

#### FR03 – Department Management
The system shall allow the creation and maintenance of departments, including the definition of a monthly budget.

#### FR04 – Expense Submission
The system shall allow employees to submit expenses associated with a valid expense category, including amount, date, description, currency, and simulated receipt.

#### FR05 – Approval Workflow
The system shall support a multi-level expense approval workflow (direct manager and finance department), as defined in a specific process flow section.

#### FR06 – Business Rules
The system shall enforce spending limits per expense category and per department, automatically generating alerts in case of violations.

#### FR07 – Alerts
The system shall allow the automatic creation, listing, filtering, and resolution of alerts by the finance department.

#### FR08 – Reports
The system shall provide expense reports by employee, category, and department, with export options in CSV and JSON formats.

---

### 3.2 Usability

#### UR01 – Ease of Use
Users shall be able to submit a basic expense with no more than 3 minutes of training.

---

### 3.3 Reliability

#### RR01 – Availability
The system shall be available at least 99% of the time during business hours.

---

### 3.4 Performance

#### PR01 – Response Time
Query operations shall present an average response time of less than 2 seconds.

---

### 3.5 Supportability

#### SR01 – Maintainability
The system shall follow clean architecture principles and design patterns to facilitate maintenance and evolution.

### 3.6 Design Constraints
